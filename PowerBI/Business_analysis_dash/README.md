# 📊 Business Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

This project presents a **Business Analysis Dashboard** built using Power BI, designed to analyze sales performance, customer contribution, and revenue distribution across regions and product categories.

The dashboard transforms raw transactional data into actionable insights to support **strategic decision-making and performance optimization**.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The dashboard provides a high-level and detailed view of:

- Revenue performance
- Customer contribution
- Regional sales distribution
- Product category performance
- Sales trends over time

It enables stakeholders to quickly identify **where revenue is coming from, who is driving it, and where improvements are needed**.

---

## 📊 Key Metrics

- **Total Revenue:** $165.7K  
- **Total Orders:** 500  
- **Total Customers:** 5  
- **Total Quantity Sold:** 1,549  

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 1. Revenue Distribution by Region
- **South region generates the highest revenue (~$44K)**
- North and West follow closely (~$41K each)
- East has the lowest (~$39K)

👉 **Insight:** Revenue is relatively balanced, but **South shows stronger performance**, making it a potential focus area for scaling.

---

### 2. Revenue by Category
- **Food leads with ~29.4% (~$48.7K)**
- Electronics contributes ~25.4%
- Clothing contributes ~23.1%
- Home contributes ~22.1%

👉 **Insight:**  
- Food is the **top-performing category**
- Other categories are closely distributed, indicating **diversified revenue streams**

---

### 3. Customer Contribution
- **Top customers (Customer_A & Customer_C)** contribute the highest revenue (~$36K & ~$35K)
- Lowest contributor generates ~$29K

👉 **Insight:**  
- Revenue is **highly concentrated among a few customers**
- Indicates **dependency risk on key customers**

---

### 4. Sales Trend Over Time
- Sales fluctuate significantly across months (Jan – June 2025)
- No clear upward or downward trend, but noticeable spikes

👉 **Insight:**  
- Business experiences **inconsistent sales patterns**
- Indicates possible **seasonality or unstable demand cycles**

---

## 💡 Business Impact & Recommendations

### ✔️ 1. Strengthen High-Performing Regions
- Focus marketing and operations on the **South region**
- Replicate successful strategies in other regions

---

### ✔️ 2. Expand High-Performing Product Category
- Increase investment in **Food category**
- Bundle or cross-sell with other categories

---

### ✔️ 3. Reduce Customer Concentration Risk
- Acquire more customers to reduce dependency on top buyers  
- Improve retention strategies for mid-level customers  

---

### ✔️ 4. Stabilize Sales Performance
- Investigate causes of sales fluctuations  
- Implement promotions during low periods  
- Improve demand forecasting
